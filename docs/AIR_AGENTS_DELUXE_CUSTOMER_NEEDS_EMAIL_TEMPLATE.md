# AIR AGENTS + Deluxe Merchant Services
## Customer-Needs Email Template

**Purpose:** A reusable outreach and follow-up email template for businesses evaluating workflow improvements, customer experience, or payment-processing options.  
**Use:** Personalize only with verified business information. Send only through an approved, consent-aware outreach process.

> **Positioning boundary:** AIR AGENTS can explore a tailored workflow, data, automation, and operations design. Deluxe Merchant Services and Eliot Management Group by Deluxe can evaluate merchant-services and payment-acceptance options. Do not imply a live integration, guaranteed savings, approval, best rate, funding timing, hardware availability, compliance status, or universal business fit without merchant-specific written confirmation.

---

## Primary discovery email

### Subject line options

1. **A simpler payment and workflow review for [Company]**
2. **A customer-first checkout conversation for [Company]**
3. **Exploring [Company]’s payment and operations priorities**
4. **One workflow worth simplifying at [Company]**

### Email body

Hi [First Name],

I’m reaching out because [Company] appears to be focused on [verified business objective, customer experience, growth initiative, or operating challenge]. Many [industry] teams are looking for ways to make the customer journey smoother while reducing the manual work behind payments, follow-up, and day-to-day operations.

Rather than assume a replacement is needed, we start with the business need: **what is working today, where the friction is, and what a better customer experience should look like.**

AIR AGENTS can help map and improve the workflow around lead intake, customer follow-up, tasks, data, and approved integrations. If payment acceptance is part of the conversation, Eliot Management Group by Deluxe can evaluate merchant-services options for in-store, online, mobile, invoicing, or remote-payment needs.

Would you be open to a brief **20-minute discovery conversation** focused on one area—for example, checkout experience, payment reporting, online payments, POS workflow, invoicing, or customer follow-up?

If it is not a priority, I’m happy to close the loop.

Best,

[Sender Name]  
[Title]  
AIR AGENTS  
[Phone] | [Email] | [Website]

## Payment-processing review variant

### Subject

**Could we review [Company]’s payment workflow?**

### Email body

Hi [First Name],

When businesses review payment processing, the biggest question usually is not simply, “What is the rate?” It is whether the full payment workflow fits the business: how customers pay, how the team operates, what systems must connect, how reporting works, and what support is needed when something goes wrong.

We can help structure that review around [Company]’s priorities. AIR AGENTS can map the surrounding workflow and required data or system connections. Eliot Management Group by Deluxe can evaluate merchant-services options for the payment side, including the channels that matter to your business.

If a pricing or processing review is useful, we would use a current written quote or a **redacted** merchant statement through an approved secure process. We do **not** request cardholder data, bank credentials, or other restricted payment information by email.

Would [two date/time options in the recipient’s time zone] work for a short needs-assessment call?

Best,

[Sender Name]

## Industry personalization modules

Use **one** relevant module after the opening paragraph. Do not stack multiple modules.

| Business type | Personalization language |
|---|---|
| **Local retail** | “For a local retail team, the practical questions are often checkout speed, POS fit, inventory workflow, staff access, gift/loyalty needs, and clear reporting across the business.” |
| **Restaurant or café** | “For food-service teams, the review usually starts with the in-person checkout experience, peak-period reliability, current POS and ordering tools, payment reporting, and the guest experience.” |
| **Professional services** | “For service businesses, remote payment, invoice payment, recurring billing, reconciliation, and client follow-up often matter more than a one-size-fits-all terminal.” |
| **E-commerce** | “For online businesses, the priority is often a reliable checkout, the right payment methods, clear reporting, customer support, and a practical fit with the current commerce platform.” |
| **Field service or mobile business** | “For mobile teams, the review can focus on taking payments wherever service happens, invoicing after the job, device preferences, reporting, and reducing follow-up delays.” |
| **Multi-location operation** | “For multi-location teams, it can be useful to map operating roles, location-level reporting, hardware requirements, support expectations, and systems that need to stay connected.” |
| **Nonprofit or events organization** | “For community organizations and events, the review can focus on online and in-person payment needs, recurring support, event workflow, reporting, and privacy controls.” |

## Reply-to-interest template

### Subject

**A few questions before we tailor the review**

Hi [First Name],

Thank you for the interest. To make the conversation useful, could you share brief answers to the questions below?

1. What is the main customer or operational problem you want to improve?
2. Which payment channels matter most: in-store, online, mobile, invoicing, recurring billing, or remote/phone payment?
3. What works well with your current process that you do not want to lose?
4. What systems must stay connected, such as POS, e-commerce, accounting, CRM, booking, or inventory tools?
5. What would make this review successful in the next 90 days?
6. Who else should be involved in the conversation?

If a detailed cost comparison becomes useful, a current written quote or securely handled **redacted** statement is more helpful than an estimate. Please do not send any payment-card data, bank credentials, tax IDs, or passwords by email.

Best,

[Sender Name]

## Send-safe checklist

- [ ] The recipient’s name, company, and business context are correct and verified.
- [ ] The message uses one customer-specific pain point or opportunity—not a feature list.
- [ ] The email contains one primary call to action.
- [ ] No claim promises savings, approval, next-day funding, free equipment, compliance, integration availability, uptime, or results.
- [ ] No cardholder data, bank information, credentials, tax IDs, or unredacted merchant statements are requested.
- [ ] Contact permission, channel preference, opt-out, and outreach policies have been honored.
- [ ] Any pricing, contract, compliance, or technical claims are supported by a current written source and routed to a qualified human owner.

## Notes for the sender

- Keep the first email under 160 words whenever possible.
- Use a respectful, consultative tone; do not imply the recipient has a problem that has not been verified.
- If the recipient declines or asks not to be contacted, stop the sequence and record the preference.
- Use **Eliot Management Group by Deluxe** as the full provider name in external-facing material unless an approved brand guideline says otherwise.
